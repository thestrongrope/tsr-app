## One page a week

This placeholder paragraph is about gathering a week of lessons on one page. It describes a small routine a student might follow during a term: set a short goal, work for a fixed time, then write two lines about what was learned.

A second paragraph keeps the page long enough to read. It suggests reviewing the notes after one day and again after one week, and marking anything still unclear.
