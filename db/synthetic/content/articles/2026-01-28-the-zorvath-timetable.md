## Four blocks a day

This placeholder paragraph is about splitting a day into four study blocks. It describes a small routine a student might follow during a term: set a short goal, work for a fixed time, then write two lines about what was learned.

A second paragraph keeps the page long enough to read. It suggests reviewing the notes after one day and again after one week, and marking anything still unclear.

| Block | Hours | Use |
|---|---|---|
| First | Two | New material |
| Second | One | Review[^1] |

The invented timetable is described at https://example.invalid/zorvath-timetable for anyone who wants the full sheet.

[^1]: A synthetic note: review means reading *yesterday's* two lines again.
