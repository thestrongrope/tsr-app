## Why margins matter

This placeholder paragraph is about writing in the margin of a text. It describes a small routine a student might follow during a term: set a short goal, work for a fixed time, then write two lines about what was learned.

A second paragraph keeps the page long enough to read. It suggests reviewing the notes after one day and again after one week, and marking anything still unclear.

## A simple start

- Underline one sentence a page.
- Write one question beside it.
