عَنْ اَبِي بَصِيرٍ عَنْ اَبِي عَبْدِ اللهِؑ قَالَ اِنَّ اللَّهَ اَجَلُّ وَ اَعْظَمُ مِنْ اَنْ يَتْرُكَ الْاَرْضَ بِغَيْرِ اِمَامٍ عَادِلٍ

Abu Baseer (r.a.) reports that Imam Abu Abdillah (peace be upon him) said, 

“Surely, Allah is more Majestic and Greater than that He leaves the earth with a Just Imam.”

## Reference

1. Al-Kaafi, vol. 1, p. 178, H. 6
