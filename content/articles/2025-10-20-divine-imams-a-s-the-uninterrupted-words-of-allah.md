عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ جُنْدَبٍ قَالَ سَاَلْتُ اَبَا الْحَسَنِؑ عَنْ قَوْلِ اللهِ عَزَّ وَ جَلَّ وَ لَقَدْ وَصَّلْنا لَهُمُ الْقَوْلَ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ قَالَ اِمَامٌ اِلَى اِمَامٍ

Abdullah Ibn Jundab reports, “I asked Imam Kazem (peace be upon him) about the word of Allah – Mighty and Majestic be He – “And indeed We have joined the ‘word’ uninterrupted perhaps they may be mindful.” He (peace be upon him) said, “One Imam to (another) Imam.”

## Note

This explanation flows without breaks. The Messenger and Imams have knowledge.
