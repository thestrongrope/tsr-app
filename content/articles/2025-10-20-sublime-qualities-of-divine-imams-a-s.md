عَنْ اَبِي هَاشِمٍ الْجَعْفَرِيِّ قَالَ سَمِعْتُ الرِّضَاؑ يَقُولُ الْاَئِمَّةُ عُلَمَاءُ حُلَمَاءُ صَادِقُونَ مُفَهَّمُونَ مُحَدَّثُونَ

Abu Haashim al-Ja’fari (r.a.) reports, “I heard Imam al-Reza (peace be upon him) say,

‘The Imams are knowledgeable, forbearing, truthful, made to understand (by Allah) and spoken to (by the angels).’”

## Note

This explanation highlights the qualities of the Imams.

## Reference

1. Behaar al-Anwaar, vol. 26, p. 66, H. 1
