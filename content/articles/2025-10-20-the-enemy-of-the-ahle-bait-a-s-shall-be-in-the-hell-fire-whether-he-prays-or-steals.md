قَالَ الصَّادِقُ ع‏ النَّاصِبُ لَنَا اَهْلَ‏ الْبَيْتِ‏ لَا يُبَالِي‏ صَامَ‏ اَوْ صَلَّى‏ اَوْ زَنَى اَوْ سَرَق‏

Imam Ja’far Sadeq (peace be upon him) said:

“For the enemies of us Ahle-Bait (peace be upon him) it does not matter whether he prays or fasts or steals and fornicates; in any case, he will be in (hell) fire.”

References:

1. Eqaab al-A’maal, p. 251, H. 18
