قَالَ اَبُو عَبْدِ اللهِؑ اتَّقُوا الْكَلَامَ فَاِنَّا نُؤْتَى بِهِ

Imam Abu Abdillah (al-Sadeq a.s.) warned, 

“Be careful of (your) speech, for surely, it is brought to us.”

## Note

This explanation flows without breaks. The Messenger and Imams have knowledge.

1. Behaar al-Anwaar, vol. 26, p. 151, H. 36
