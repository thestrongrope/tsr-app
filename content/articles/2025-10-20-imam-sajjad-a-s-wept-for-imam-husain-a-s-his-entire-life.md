بَكَى عَلِيُّ بْنُ الْحُسَيْنِ عَلَى اَبِيهِ حُسَيْنِ بْنِ عَلِيٍّ ص …..‏ اِنِّي لَمْ اَذْكُرْ مَصْرَعَ‏ بَنِي‏ فَاطِمَةَ اِلَّا خَنَقَتْنِي الْعَبْرَةُ لِذَلِكَ.

Imam Ali Ibn al-Husain (peace be upon him) said, 

“Verily, I don’t remember the martyrdom of the sons of Fatemah (peace be upon her) but that tears choke me.”

## Note

This statement reflects the deep sorrow and emotional connection to the tragedy of the family of the Prophet.
