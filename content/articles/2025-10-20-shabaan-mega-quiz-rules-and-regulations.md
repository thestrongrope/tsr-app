The Strong Ropes (A.S.) team brings new video series for its viewers named “Imam Mahdi (a.t.f.s.) : Panaah-e-Kainaat” Mega Quiz from 6th Shabaan to 20th Shabaan. In this video series and Mega Quiz we will come up with 15 new episodes for our viewers which will be in reference to Imam Mahdi (a.t.f.s.) and the crux of the matter will be that all these discussions/conversations will be verified basis the books which are written by Ahle Tasannun scholars.

The way to participate in the Mega Quiz which will be after the video series:

- Firstly register for the Quiz on this link: [https://account.thestrongrope.com/](https://account.thestrongrope.com/)

- This registration link will also be available in the description and the comments of the video.

- If the link does not open then copy it and open it in a different browser, Insha’Allah it will work for sure.

- After the link opens you get to see the G icon which is clickable and please sign in with your Gmail ID through which you want to participate in the Quiz.

- The moment you register you can see the Token number and your details.

- On the same page, you get an option to Edit your profile.

- Please fill in all your details correctly especially Name, Contact Number, and Address as we will contact you through these details only in case you are the winner by the grace of Imam (A.S.)

Now let us see how this Mega Quiz will be conducted:

- Every day starting from 6th to 20th of Shabaan at 9.30 PM IST a video will be relayed on our YT channel ([https://www.youtube.com/c/TheStrongRope](https://www.youtube.com/c/TheStrongRope)).

- Every episode will have questions related to that video.

- The link for the Quiz will be present in the comment and the description of the same video.

- You are required to pay attention to the episode and give answers as quickly as possible.

This Mega quiz is slightly different from our other Grand Quiz. To win a prize there are 2 ways to it:

- Lucky Draw

- Top Scorer basis how fast you answer.

- The daily top scorer will be announced in the next subsequent episode.

- In this 15 episode video series after the completion of the Mega Quiz 100 top scorers will be selected basis correct answers and how fast the quiz was attempted.

- 1st prize winner will be basis our computerized lucky draw system of these top 100 scorers.

- 1st prize winner will get an opportunity to go for Iraq’s Ziyarat which will consist of a ticket and visa to Iraq.

- Hence it is expected that you watch all our video series and answer as quickly as possible.

- 2nd and 3rd prize will not be as per lucky draw but it will completely be on the basis of the score.

- The top 5 scorers who answer questions asked in all the 15 episodes very quickly will be awarded the 2nd prize of Amazon Voucher of Rs 5,000 each.

- The next top 14 scorers basis correct answers and the lowest time taken to submit their answers will get the 3rd prize of Rs 2,500 Amazon Gift Voucher.

- But if you are not amongst the top scorer then also 15 people stand a chance to win the 4th prize of Rs 1,400 Amazon Gift Voucher which will be allocated basis lucky draw.

- Apart from this every day one winner will be announced as per lucky draw which means 15 winners for 15 days of Rs 1,400 Amazon Gift Voucher.

- This means in total Imam Mahdi (a.t.f.s.): Panaah-e-Kainaat Mega Quiz will have 50 winners Insha’Allah.
