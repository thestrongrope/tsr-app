عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ حُبُّ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ يَوْمًا خَيْرٌ مِنْ عِبَادَةِ سَنَةٍ وَ مَنْ مَاتَ عَلَيْهِ دَخَلَ الْجَنَّةَ 

The Holy Prophet (peace be on him and his progeny) declared,

“The love of Aale Muhammad (peace be on them) for a day is better than the worship of a year and whoever dies upon it will enter paradise.”

## Note

1) Worship means fasting during the day and praying during the night  
2) Love without expressing disgust against their enemies is meaningless on the basis of several traditions  
3) One cannot claim to love the Ahle Bait (peace be on them) while hating their Shias like many spiteful opponents do  

## Reference

1. Kashf al-Ghummah, vol. 1, p. 137 narrating from Musnad of Ahmad Ibn Hanbal  
2. Kashf al-Yaqeen, p. 225  
3. Behaar al-Anwaar, vol. 27, p. 104, H. 72
