عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ سِنَانٍ عَنِ الصَّادِقِؑ اَنَّهٗ قَالَ اِنَّ لِلهِ عَزَّ وَ جَلَّ حُرُمَاتٍ ثَلَاثًا لَيْسَ مِثْلَهُنَّ شَيْ‏ءٌ كِتَابُهٗ وَ هُوَ حِكْمَتُهٗ وَ نُوْرُهٗ وَ بَيْتُهُ الَّذِيْ جَعَلَهٗ قِبْلَةً لِلنَّاسِ لَا يَقْبَلُ مِنْ اَحَدٍ تَوَجُّهًا اِلٰى غَيْرِهٖ وَ عِتْرَةُ نَبِيِّكُمْ ﷺ

Abdullah Ibn Sinaan reports that Imam Sadeq (peace be upon him) said, 

“Surely, for Allah – Mighty and Majestic be He – there are three sacrosanct things, nothing is equal to them. His Book, which is His Wisdom and His Light; His House, which He has made as a Qiblah for the people and if anyone turns in any other direction than it, Allah will not accept from him; and the progeny of your Prophet (peace be upon him and his holy progeny).”

## Reference

2. Ma’ani al-Akhbaar, p. 117
