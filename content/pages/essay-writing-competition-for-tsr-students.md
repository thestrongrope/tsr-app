بِسْمِ اللہِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِ

وَ صَلَّی اللہُ عَلَیْکَ یَا وَلِیَّ الْعَصْرِ (عج) اَدْرِکْنَا وَ لَا تُھْلِکْنَا

قَالَ الإمام الرِّضَا (عليه السلام) حَدَّثَنِي أَبِي عَنْ أَبِيهِ قَالَ إِنَّ يَوْمَ الْغَدِيرِ فِي السَّمَاءِ أَشْهَرُ مِنْهُ فِي الْأَرْضِ

Imam Reza (peace be upon him) says that my father narrated to me from his father, who said:

**&#8220;The Day of Ghadeer is more famous in the heavens than on the earth.&#8221;**

[Wasaael al-Shiah, vol.14, H. 19442]

Insha’Allah the joyous occasion of Ghadeer will soon be upon us. This blessed day offers us a precious opportunity to reaffirm our love and devotion to Ameerul Momineen Imam Ali Ibn Abi Talib (peace be upon them both). One novel way to make this pious day more memorable is to pen down our thoughts about this grand occasion and offer them in the services of Ahle Bayt (peace be upon them), especially Ameerul Momineen (peace be upon him).

COMPETITION DETAILS

**ELIGIBILITY**: All TSR Students are eligible to participate in the essay writing competition

**PRIZES:** 5 Best Essay writers shall receive a Cash Prize of INR 1800 each

**LAST DATE TO SUBMIT ESSAY:** 9-Jun-2024 (Sunday) &#8211; *the submission form is available below*

LIST OF TOPICS FOR GHADEER ESSAY WRITING COMPETITION

- Quranic Verses revealed about the incident of Ghadeer

- Authenticity of the Hadith of Ghadeer

- Why was the Sermon of Ghadeer delivered in Khum and not in Mecca?

- Why is Ghadeer celebrated as an Eid?

- Consequences of denying Ghadeer

ESSAY WRITING GUIDELINES

- The essays should be written in English or Urdu and between 1000-1500 words (kindly refrain from submission in Roman Urdu)

- Kindly quote complete references everywhere (Book, Volume, Page No., Hadith No.)

- If you are quoting anybody, kindly quote the complete reference there also

- Please avoid WhatsApp language or emojis

All in His (a.t.f.s.) service!
