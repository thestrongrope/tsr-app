**Last updated: 10 October 2026**

This policy explains what the The Strong Rope website (thestrongrope.com), the
member site (account.thestrongrope.com) and the The Strong Rope mobile app for
iOS and Android collect, why, and what you can do about it. All three are run
by **The Strong Rope** ("we", "us"). We decide what is collected and why, and
we are responsible for your personal data.

Part A covers the website. Part B covers the mobile app. Part C covers the
member site. The sections after them apply to all three.

---

## Summary

- You do **not** need an account to read the website.
- You **do** need an account to use the mobile app or the member site, and we
  collect your email, name and the profile details you choose to give us.
- The website and the member site use Google Analytics; the app uses Firebase
  Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to see when it crashes and how it is used.
- We do not show advertising, we do not collect an advertising ID, and we do
  not sell your data.

---

## Part A — The website

### A1. Information collected automatically

**Google Analytics.** The site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages
are read and how people arrive. Google sets cookies on your device and receives
your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view and the site or
search that referred you. This information is processed by Google on servers
outside your country. **We do not show a cookie banner, and we do not ask for
your consent first — Google Analytics loads and sets its cookies as soon as a
page opens.** You can block it with your browser's cookie settings or a browser
extension.

**YouTube thumbnails.** The home page and the lectures list show preview images
for our YouTube videos. Those images are loaded straight from Google's servers
(`i.ytimg.com`) rather than from ours, so opening either page sends your IP
address and the address of the page you are on to Google, whether or not you
click anything. We do not embed the YouTube player itself, so YouTube's own
player cookies are not set unless you follow a link to YouTube.

**Server logs and rate limiting.** Our servers see your IP address with each
request. It is used to serve the page, to apply rate limits that protect the
site from abuse, and to diagnose faults. We do not use it to build a profile of
you.

### A2. Search analytics

When you search articles or lectures, we record the search terms, how many
results came back, how long the search took, and which result you clicked. This
is stored on our own servers and is **anonymous by design** — no IP address, no
cookies and no browser identifier are recorded with it. We use it to find
searches that return nothing useful and improve the library.

### A3. Information kept on your device

Some preferences are saved in your browser's local storage and never leave your
device or reach our servers:

- your light or dark theme choice
- anything you type into the self-reflection worksheet

Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

### A4. What the website does **not** do

- It does **not** have visitor accounts, sign-in or registration.
- It does **not** sell anything or take payments.
- It does **not** run advertising, ad networks or remarketing pixels.
- It does **not** ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

## Part B — The mobile app

### B1. Account information

To create and use an account we collect and store:

| Data | Required | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Yes | You, or your Google account if you use Google Sign-In |
| Name | Yes | You, or your Google account |
| Profile picture | No | Your Google account, or a picture you set |
| Gender | No | You |
| Age range | No | You |
| Mobile number | No | You |
| Country, state, city, postal code | No | You |
| Preferred language | No | You |
| Contact/update preferences | No | You |

Your role within the organisation (for example student, teacher or
administrator) is also stored so the app can show the right features.

### B2. Authentication

Sign-in is handled by **Google Firebase Authentication**. You can sign in with
an email address and password, or with **Google Sign-In**. When you use Google
Sign-In, Google shares your email address, name and profile picture with us.

We never see or store your Google password. Authentication tokens are stored on
your device in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore.

### B3. Device information

Each request the app makes to our servers includes:

- the app version
- your device model (and manufacturer on Android)
- your operating system version

We use this to support the app, investigate faults and decide which devices and
OS versions to keep supporting. It is not used to build an advertising profile.

### B4. Push notifications

If you allow notifications, **Firebase Cloud Messaging** issues a token
identifying your installation of the app, and we store it so we can send you
notifications about new content and class announcements. If you turn
notifications off, the token is no longer used to reach you.

### B5. Content you create

If you use the class, syllabus or quiz features, we store your answers,
attendance and related records so the app can show your progress and so your
teachers and administrators can see the results for their classes.

### B6. Crash reports and app usage

We use two Google Firebase services to keep the app working:

**Firebase Crashlytics** records crashes so we can fix them. A crash report
contains the error and the sequence of code that led to it, your device model,
operating system version, app version, and an identifier for your installation
of the app.

**Firebase Analytics** tells us how the app is used: which screens are opened,
how often the app is launched, session length, device and operating system,
and an approximate region derived from your IP address.

We use this to fix faults and decide what to improve. We do **not** use it to
advertise to you, and we do **not** link it to your name or email address.

**We have turned off collection of the advertising ID.** The app is used by
students and may be used by children, so we do not collect that identifier
even though the service would otherwise do so by default.

### B7. What the app does **not** collect

- It does **not** show advertising, and it does **not** collect an advertising
  ID.
- It does **not** collect your location. Country, state, city and postal code
  come only from what you type into your profile.
- It does **not** access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

## Part C — The member site

The member site (account.thestrongrope.com) is where members, teachers and
administrators manage classes, registrations and quizzes in a web browser.

### C1. Your account

You sign in with the same account as the app. The account information in B1,
the sign-in described in B2 and the records described in B5 apply to the
member site too. The site sets a session cookie to keep you signed in.

If you allow notifications in your browser, Firebase Cloud Messaging issues a
token for that browser, and we store it to send you notifications as described
in B4.

### C2. Google Analytics

The member site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages are used.
Google sets cookies on your device and receives your IP address, browser and
device type, the pages you view and the page that referred you. This
information is processed by Google on servers outside your country.

When a page opens we send Google its address **without** the part after the
`?`, because that part can contain class codes and other details that relate
to you. A few administrator report pages later add their filter choices (dates,
class level, class and gender) to the address, and those can reach Google.
Google signals and ad personalisation are turned off, and we do **not**
link analytics to your name or email address.

**We do not show a cookie banner, and we do not ask for your consent first —
Google Analytics loads and sets its cookies as soon as a page opens.** You can
block it with your browser's cookie settings or a browser extension.

### C3. Server logs

Our servers see your IP address with each request. It is used to serve the
page, to keep the site secure and to diagnose faults. For administrators, we
also record which pages they open, with their IP address and browser, as an
audit trail.

## The website, the member site and the app

### 1. Why we use your information

- To create your account and sign you in (app).
- To show you content and features appropriate to your role (app).
- To record class attendance, syllabus progress and quiz results (app).
- To send notifications you have agreed to receive (app).
- To understand which content is read and searched for, and which member-site
  pages are used, so we can improve them.
- To see when the app crashes and how it is used, so we can fix faults and
  decide what to improve (app).
- To keep the service secure and to diagnose faults.

### 2. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the partners and service providers who help us
run the website, the member site and the app:

| Recipient | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Google Analytics | Website and member-site audience measurement |
| YouTube / Google (`i.ytimg.com`) | Delivering video thumbnail images to your browser on the website |
| Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Messaging) | App and member-site sign-in and push notifications |
| Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics) | App crash reports and app usage |
| Our hosting and technology providers | Running our servers and storing account, class, content and search-analytics data |

Our hosting and technology providers act on our instructions and may not use
your data for their own purposes. Google processes website analytics data under
its own terms, as described in Parts A and C.

Your data may be processed outside your country, including on servers operated
by Google. We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to
respond to a lawful request, or to protect our rights.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not sell
or rent your personal data to anyone.

### 3. How long we keep it

We keep your app account information while your account exists. Class,
attendance and quiz records are kept while you are enrolled and afterwards for
our own record-keeping.

When you ask us to delete your account, we anonymise your personal details and
keep the class, attendance and quiz records without anything identifying you,
so class statistics stay accurate. We keep anything the law requires us to
keep.

Anonymous website search analytics are not linked to any person and are kept for
as long as they are useful for improving the site.

### 4. Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction** — view and edit your profile in the app or on the
  member site.
- **Notifications** — turn them off in your device settings at any time.
- **Contact preferences** — change your update and unsubscribe settings in your
  profile.
- **Website tracking** — block or clear cookies in your browser settings to stop
  Google Analytics on the website and the member site.
- **Deletion** — you can ask us to delete your account and personal data by
  emailing thestrongrope@gmail.com. Because we are an international,
  volunteer-run group, this can take up to **60 days**. If we still owe you a
  prize, we keep the details needed to deliver it until delivery is complete,
  and finish the deletion afterwards. We will tell you if that applies to you.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to a copy of your data,
to object to certain processing, or to complain to a data protection regulator.

### 5. Children

The website and the app are intended for general audiences, and the app is used
by students of the organisation. We do not knowingly collect personal data from
children in a way that requires parental consent under applicable law. If you
believe a child has given us personal data without appropriate consent, contact
us and we will remove it.

### 6. Security

App authentication tokens are stored using the iOS Keychain and the Android
Keystore. Traffic between the website, the app and our servers uses HTTPS. No
system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your
information.

### 7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, where the
change is significant, tell you in the app or on the website.
